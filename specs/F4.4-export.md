# F4.4 Export (text & PDF)

**Phase** 4 · **Size** S · **Prototype** no · **Requirements** FR-8 (link-free sharing) · **Depends on** F3.1

## Summary
Share a recipe without a link: copy it as text, or export a PDF. Works offline, and works in the prototype if moved forward.

## Behavior
The recipe's ⋯ menu has **Copy as text** and **Export PDF**.

- **Copy as text:** puts a plain-text version on the clipboard, then shows a "Copied" toast.
  ```
  Garlic Spinach Chicken (serves 2 · 30 min)

  Ingredients
  - 300 g chicken thighs
  - 200 g spinach, roughly chopped
  ...

  Steps
  1. ...

  Per serving: ~520 kcal · 38 g protein · 41 g carbs · 22 g fat
  AI-generated recipe: check that meat reaches a safe temperature.
  ```
- **Export PDF:**
  - Renders a print layout (A4/Letter, one column, title, meta line, ingredients, steps, macros, disclaimer) with SwiftUI `ImageRenderer` into a PDF context.
  - Pages break across steps where needed.
  - Saved to a temporary file and opened in the share sheet (Save to Files, Print, AirDrop, Mail). The temporary file is deleted after the share sheet closes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Copied text pastes cleanly into Notes and Messages.
- [ ] The PDF has selectable text and paginates a 15-step recipe correctly.
- [ ] Both work in airplane mode.
- [ ] Both include the food-safety disclaimer.
