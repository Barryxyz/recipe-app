# F5.3 App Store readiness

**Phase** 5 · **Size** M · **Prototype** no · **Requirements** NFR-7, D5 · **Depends on** everything in v1

## Summary
Everything needed to pass App Review and launch to the first 100 users safely.

## Checklist
### Accounts & programs
- [ ] Apple Developer Program ($99/year). Bundle ID, Sign in with Apple and Associated Domains capabilities.
- [ ] **Paid Apps Agreement** signed in App Store Connect, with tax forms and bank details. A paid app can't go on sale without it.
- [ ] Enrolled in the **App Store Small Business Program**, so the commission is 15% instead of 30%. Apply before launch.
- [ ] Price set to **$4.99** (US), with Apple's automatic prices for other countries.
- [ ] `REQUIRE_PURCHASE=true` and `ALLOW_XCODE=false` in prod (F0.6).
- [ ] Supabase prod project on Pro ($25/month), with daily backups.
- [ ] **Anthropic account monthly spend limit set** before anyone other than the developer is invited (D5).

### Privacy
- [ ] **Privacy policy** at `<share-domain>/privacy` (F4.2). It says:
  - Photos are sent to Anthropic's API to identify food and aren't stored by the app.
  - What account, pantry and recipe data is stored, and where (Supabase).
  - How to delete an account.
  - Anthropic's API data retention terms, **checked against Anthropic's current commercial terms** at launch.
- [ ] **Third-party AI disclosure and consent:** App Review guidelines require clearly disclosing, and getting permission before, sending personal data to a third-party AI. Covered by the onboarding notice (F1.7) plus a one-time consent sheet before the **first scan** and the **first generation**. Check against the current guidelines at submission.
- [ ] **App Privacy labels:** user ID, product interaction, diagnostics, and "other user content" (pantry, recipes). Decide with the policy text whether transient photo processing counts as "collected".
- [ ] In-app **Delete account** works end to end (F0.2).
- [ ] Camera usage string (`NSCameraUsageDescription`): "Take photos of your fridge so we can list your ingredients."

### Content & safety
- [ ] Food-safety disclaimer on recipe detail, export and the shared page.
- [ ] Allergen note in onboarding, and the allergen test from F2.2 passing.
- [ ] Nutrition marked as approximate.
- [ ] Age rating questionnaire filled in (expected 4+).

### Release
- [ ] App icon, launch screen, 6.9" and 6.5" screenshots, description and keywords. There's no free trial, so these have to sell the app.
- [ ] TestFlight internal build, then an external group (needs Beta App Review) for the first testers.
- [ ] Review notes explain that no login is needed (anonymous accounts), so App Review can test the full app. App Review's builds give Sandbox proof, which prod accepts (F0.6). Confirm this on the first TestFlight build.
- [ ] Go/no-go metrics from the prototype are recorded in DESIGN §12: scan accuracy, recipe quality, cost per active user.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The app passes App Review on the first or second submission.
- [ ] The spend limit is confirmed in the Anthropic console.
- [ ] The privacy policy URL works and matches the privacy labels.
