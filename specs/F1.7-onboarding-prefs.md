# F1.7 Onboarding & preferences

**Phase** 1 · **Size** S · **Prototype** yes (simple version) · **Requirements** FR-12 · **Depends on** F0.2, F0.4

## Summary
A short, skippable onboarding that collects diets, allergens, staples and default servings, which recipe generation uses. Everything can be edited later in Settings.

## Flow (4 screens, with Skip on each)
1. **Welcome:** a one-line explanation, plus a notice that photos are sent to an AI service to identify food and are not stored (this also covers F5.3's third-party AI disclosure). Button: **Get started**.
2. **Diet** (multi-select chips): vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, keto, low-carb, gluten-free, dairy-free, halal, kosher. Default: none.
3. **Allergies** (multi-select): milk, egg, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat/gluten, soy, sesame, plus free-text **Other**. A note below says: "We'll avoid these in recipes, but always check labels."
4. **Staples:** "Do you usually have these?" Catalog items with `is_common_staple`, pre-checked. Search lets the user add more. Plus a **Servings** stepper (default 2).

Finish → the empty pantry screen, offering "Scan your fridge" or "Add manually".

## Data
- Saves to `app.user_prefs`: `diets`, `allergens`, `staple_ingredient_ids`, `default_servings`.
- "Onboarding complete" is stored locally. Reinstalling with an account that has prefs skips onboarding.

## Settings
Settings → Food preferences shows the same three sections as editable lists.

## How allergens are enforced
- **Prompt:** the allergens are listed as hard exclusions (F2.2).
- **Code check (the important part):** every recipe ingredient's `catalog.ingredients.allergens` is checked against the user's allergens.
  - Tags come from `catalog.ingredients.allergens` (DESIGN §5, seeded in F0.4).
  - Free-text "Other" allergens and custom items can't be checked by tags. For those, a case-insensitive name match on the ingredient name, plus the prompt, is the best effort.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Onboarding can be finished or skipped in under 30 s.
- [ ] Prefs are saved and visible in Settings.
- [ ] Staples appear as available ingredients in recipe generation.
- [ ] The allergy screen shows the "always check labels" note.
