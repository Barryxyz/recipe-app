# F1.1 Photo scan (capture & upload)

**Phase** 1 · **Size** M · **Prototype** yes · **Requirements** FR-1 · **Depends on** F0.2, F0.5 · **Next** F1.2, F1.3

## Summary
The user takes 1–4 photos of a fridge, freezer or pantry. The app shrinks them and sends them to `scan-ingredients`. Photos stay in memory only and are never stored.

## User stories
- As a user, I can take several photos of different shelves, so items at the back are included.
- As a user, I can choose which storage location I'm scanning, so expiry dates are right.
- As a user who denied camera access, I'm pointed to manual entry instead of a dead end.

## Flow
1. Entry points: the **Scan** button on the pantry screen and "Scan your fridge" on the empty pantry screen.
2. **Location picker:** Fridge / Freezer / Pantry, defaulting to the last one used. It is a segmented control at the top of the camera view.
3. **Camera:** a custom `AVCaptureSession` view with a shutter button and a thumbnail strip of the photos taken (tap a thumbnail to remove it).
   - Maximum 4 photos. The shutter is disabled at 4, with the hint "Max 4 photos".
   - A **Library** button imports from Photos (`PhotosPicker`). This helps when testing with saved fridge photos.
4. **Scan (n)** sends the photos. While it runs, the app shows a progress screen ("Looking for ingredients…", typically 8–20 s) with **Cancel**.
5. When the response arrives, the review screen opens (F1.3).

## Image processing (on device)
- Resize so the longest edge is 1568 px, encode as JPEG at quality 0.8, and strip all EXIF data, including location.
- Each photo should end up at ≤ 500 KB.
- Request: `POST /scan-ingredients` with `{ location, images: [base64 jpeg] }`.
- Release image data from memory once the response arrives or the user cancels.

## Errors
| Case | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Camera permission denied | Explain why the camera is needed, with **Open Settings** and **Add manually instead** (F1.5) |
| Offline | "Scanning needs an internet connection", with **Add manually** (which works offline in v1) |
| Timeout or `ai_failed` | "Couldn't read the photos", with **Retry**. Photos are kept in memory until the user leaves the screen. |
| `ai_disabled` | Banner, plus a manual-entry fallback |

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Can capture 1–4 photos, remove one, and pick a location.
- [ ] The uploaded JPEGs are ≤ 1568 px and have no EXIF (checked in a debug build).
- [ ] Nothing is written to disk: no files in the app container after a scan, and nothing added to the camera roll.
- [ ] Cancelling mid-request makes no further UI changes. The server call may still finish and be logged.
- [ ] Denying camera permission leads to manual entry in one tap.

## Out of scope
Live detection in the camera preview, video, barcode scanning.
