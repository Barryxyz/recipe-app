# F3.5 Shopping list

**Phase** 3 · **Size** M · **Prototype** yes · **Requirements** FR-10 · **Depends on** F1.5, F1.6, F2.4 · **Design** §6.4

## Summary
A household shopping list. Items come from recipes (missing ingredients and amounts the pantry is short on) or are added by hand. Bought items can move straight into the pantry.

## Adding from a recipe
**Add missing to shopping list**, on any recipe, collects:
- ingredients marked `missing` (generated recipes), or, for saved and manual recipes, ingredients not covered by active pantry items or staples at the moment the button is tapped
- pantry ingredients where the recipe needs more than is on hand (same conversion as the F2.2 quantity check), adding only the **shortfall**

A preview sheet lists them, each with a checkbox, before they're added. **Merge rule:** if an unchecked list item with the same `ingredient_id` and a compatible unit exists, increase its quantity. Otherwise add a new row. `source_recipe_id` is set.

Toast: "4 items added to shopping list".

## Shopping tab
- **Sections:** To buy, grouped by category (store aisle order: produce, meat/fish, dairy, bakery, pantry, frozen, other), and **Bought** (collapsed, checked items).
- **Row:** checkbox, name, quantity and unit, and a small "for <recipe title>" label when it came from a recipe.
- **Add:** a text field at the top. It uses the same catalog search as F1.5, and free text is allowed, which stores the item with `ingredient_id = null`. Return adds it and keeps the field focused.
- **Swipe** deletes. Tap edits the quantity and unit.

## Checking off → pantry
- Checking an item moves it to **Bought**.
- The **Bought** section header has **Add to pantry (n)**, which opens a sheet of the checked items.
  - Each item has a location (default from category, as in F1.5) and the quantity bought, prefilled from the list.
  - Expiry follows F1.6.
- **Confirm:** inserts `pantry_items` with `source = 'shopping_list'` and deletes those list rows, in one RPC.
- **Clear bought** deletes checked items without adding them to the pantry.

## Implementation notes (2026-11-10)
Approved during review:
- **Shopping tab**, third after Pantry and Recipes, with a badge for items to buy.
- **"for <recipe>"** uses a new `source_recipe_title` column, so it also works for recipe ideas that weren't saved.
- **On hand:** staples and items without an amount ("some") always count as on hand; expired items don't count. Amounts compare with the Cooked this conversion (F3.4); an amount that can't be compared counts as enough. Optional ingredients are never added. Amounts to buy are rounded up (whole pieces, whole grams).
- **Merging:** by catalog id, or by name for free text, into an unchecked row whose unit converts; otherwise a new row.
- **Typed items:** a close catalog match (score ≥ 0.6) is linked on Return; anything else is free text and becomes a custom ingredient when moved to the pantry.
- **Deleting** is a hard delete (no list history). No live sync between household members in the prototype: the list reloads on open and pull to refresh.
- **Button:** "Add missing to shopping list (n)" under Cooked this, hidden when nothing is needed.
- **Database:** `app.add_shopping_items(items)` (adds or merges) and `app.move_bought_to_pantry(items)` (pantry items + removes rows), one transaction each.
- Code: `Features/Shopping/` (`ShoppingPlan`, `ShoppingListView`, `ShoppingSheets`), `Services/ShoppingAPI.swift`, `RecipeDetailView`, `RootView`; tests: `ShoppingPlanTests`, `supabase/tests/shopping_list_test.sql`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Adding missing items from the same recipe twice merges the quantities instead of making duplicates.
- [x] Shortfall is right: the recipe needs 400 g, the pantry has 150 g, so 250 g is added.
- [x] Free-text items work, and become custom items when moved to the pantry.
- [x] **Add to pantry** creates pantry items with correct expiry and removes them from the list.
- [x] The list is shared by the household (one member in v1). RLS is by `household_id`.
