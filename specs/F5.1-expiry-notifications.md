# F5.1 Expiry notifications

**Phase** 5 · **Size** S · **Prototype** local only (in scope per DESIGN §9) · **Requirements** gap table "Expiry alerts" · **Depends on** F1.4, F1.6

## Summary
At most one local notification a day, listing what's about to expire. There's no push server.

## Behavior
- **Permission:** requested in context, right after the user's **first pantry save** (scan or manual), with a short explanation first. It's never asked on first launch.
- **Content:** "3 items expire soon: milk (today), spinach, chicken thighs (tomorrow)". Up to 3 names, then "+2 more".
  - Expired items get one mention, on the day after they expire, then no more.
- **Tap:** opens the pantry on "All" with the Expiring soon section expanded.
- **Scheduling:**
  - Local notifications are scheduled in advance. Whenever the pantry changes, and on each launch or foreground, cancel and reschedule digests for the **next 7 days**, using current pantry data.
  - A day with nothing expiring gets no notification.
  - `BGAppRefreshTask` reschedules about daily, so the digests stay current if the app isn't opened.
  - This stays far under iOS's 64 pending-notification limit.
- **Settings:**
  - Notifications on or off
  - Time (default 9:00)
  - "Warn me" lead time: 1 / 2 / 3 days (default 2)

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] With items expiring tomorrow, a digest arrives at the chosen time.
- [ ] Marking an item used before the digest time removes it from that digest.
- [ ] No notification on days with nothing expiring.
- [ ] Turning notifications off cancels every pending one.
