# F3.3 Delete recipe

**Phase** 3 · **Size** S · **Prototype** yes · **Requirements** FR-7 · **Depends on** F3.1

## Summary
Delete a saved recipe, with a confirmation and a short undo window.

## Behavior
1. **Entry points:** swipe left on a list row, or **Delete** in the recipe's ⋯ menu.
2. **Confirmation alert:** "Delete <title>?"
   - If the recipe is shared, it adds: "Its share link will stop working."
   - Buttons: **Delete** (destructive) and **Cancel**.
3. The row is removed from the list right away, and a **Recipe deleted · Undo** toast shows for 5 s.
4. **The delete is committed** when any of the following happens. In the prototype that's `delete from recipes`, which cascades to ingredients. In v1 it's a soft delete: set `deleted_at` and clear `share_token` (F3.6).
   - the toast expires, or
   - the app goes to the background, or
   - another delete starts.
5. **Undo** before the commit puts the row back. Nothing was sent to the server.

## Implementation notes (2026-11-09, built with F3.1)
Approved during review: swipe left → **Delete** with an undo toast, like the pantry (no confirmation alert; Undo covers mistakes).
- The delete is a **soft delete right away** (`deleted_at`), and **Undo** clears it. Simpler than a delayed commit and safe if the app is closed during the 5 s: the recipe just stays deleted, as the spec requires for backgrounding.
- The ⋯ menu entry comes with the recipe menu (F3.2). Share links (v1) are handled with F4.1.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Undo within 5 s leaves the recipe untouched on the server.
- [ ] After 5 s, or when the app is backgrounded, the recipe is deleted (prototype) or soft-deleted (v1) and no longer appears anywhere in the app.
- [ ] Deleting a shared recipe makes its link return 404 (v1).
- ~~Deleting frees a slot under the 50 limit.~~ No limit any more (F3.1).
