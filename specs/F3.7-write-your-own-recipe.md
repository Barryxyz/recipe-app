# F3.7 Write your own recipe

**Phase** 3 · **Size** M · **Prototype** yes · **Requirements** FR-14 · **Depends on** F3.1, F3.2 (editor), F2.3, F1.5 (search), F0.4 · **Design** §6.3a

## Summary
Users can write recipes from scratch, such as family recipes or ones from a cookbook, with their own ingredients, description and steps. It reuses the recipe editor from F3.2 in "create" mode. Writing a recipe never calls the AI. The only AI use is a small nutrition estimate for ingredients the catalog doesn't know.

## User stories
- As a user, I can save my own recipe so it sits alongside the AI ones.
- As a user copying a recipe from a website or note, I can paste the ingredient list and steps instead of typing each line.
- As a user, I see macros for my recipe, calculated the same way as for AI recipes.
- As a user, I can cook, share, export and shop for my recipe like any other.

## Entry points
- **+** on the Recipes tab → **New recipe**.
- Recipes empty state: **Write your own**, next to **Generate recipes**.
- **Duplicate** in any saved recipe's ⋯ menu opens a filled-in copy as a new recipe. A copy of a recipe that is `manual` stays `manual`. A copy of an AI or shared recipe is saved as `edited`.

## Form
The F3.2 editor, empty, with these differences:

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | Required, ≤ 80 characters |
| Description | Optional, ≤ 300 characters |
| Servings | Default `user_prefs.default_servings`. No scaling prompt while creating. |
| Prep / cook minutes | Optional. Blank is stored as null and shown as "—". |
| Ingredients | At least 1. Each line: name (catalog search from F1.5), quantity or "some", unit, note, optional toggle. |
| Steps | At least 1, none empty. Can be reordered. |

**Ingredient names:**
- Picking a catalog result sets `ingredient_id`.
- A name with no match is saved as a custom ingredient (`ingredients_private`, nutrition null), the same as manual pantry entry, so its macros can be estimated later.

### Paste ingredients
**Paste list** opens a text box. An on-device parser turns each line into an ingredient row:
- **Quantity:** a leading number, including `1 1/2`, `½` and `1-2` (takes the higher number).
- **Unit:** the next word, if it's a known unit or an abbreviation or plural of one (`tbsp`, `Tbsp`, `tablespoons`, `g`, `cups`, …).
- **Name:** the rest of the line. Text after the first comma becomes the note ("onion, finely diced").
- Bullets, numbering and blank lines are ignored.

Each parsed line is matched to the catalog (top result with score ≥ 0.6). The lines appear as editable rows, and unmatched rows are tagged "Custom". No AI is used.

### Paste steps
**Paste steps** splits the text into steps at numbered lines (`1.`, `2)`, `Step 3`) or, failing that, at blank lines. Numbering is removed from the text.

## Save
1. RPC `app.save_recipe` with `source = 'manual'` and `model_version = null`. It counts toward the 50-recipe limit, with the F3.1 replace flow at 50 (v1).
2. `recompute-macros` (F2.3) runs after saving.
   - Custom ingredients with no nutrition get a one-time estimate. That's an AI call, so it needs a verified purchase (F0.6).
   - If the estimate isn't available (unverified account, offline or AI disabled), the macros show "≈" with "Some ingredients have no nutrition data" and are filled in later automatically.
3. The new recipe opens in the detail view.

**Draft:** the form autosaves locally. Leaving with unsaved content asks "Keep draft / Discard". A kept draft reopens from **New recipe**.

**Offline (v1):** saving works offline (F3.6), and macros are calculated after sync.

## Allergens & diets
If an ingredient matches the user's allergens (catalog tags or a name match, as in F2.2), show a **warning** banner: "Contains peanuts, which is on your allergy list." Unlike AI recipes, this doesn't block saving, because it's the user's own recipe.

## How manual recipes differ in the app
- **List (F3.1):** a "Mine" tag. Search, filters and "Can make now" work the same.
- **Detail view:** no "AI-generated" disclaimer. Macros are still marked approximate.
- **Editing (F3.2):** the recipe stays `manual`.
- **Cooked this, shopping list, sharing, export:** unchanged. Pantry, staple and missing marks are worked out against the current pantry when the recipe is viewed (F3.5).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A recipe with a title, 3 ingredients and 2 steps can be created and saved, and it appears in the list with the "Mine" tag.
- [ ] Pasting this list produces 4 correct rows: `2 cups flour`, `1 1/2 tsp salt`, `3 eggs, beaten`, `½ cup milk`.
- [ ] Pasting numbered steps produces one step per number, without the numbers.
- [ ] An unknown ingredient becomes a custom ingredient and gets estimated macros (when verified).
- [ ] Saving without a title, an ingredient or a step shows inline errors.
- [ ] An allergen in the recipe shows a warning but doesn't block saving.
- [ ] Creating a recipe makes no AI call unless a custom ingredient needs nutrition.
- [ ] Duplicate of an AI recipe saves as `edited`. Duplicate of a manual recipe saves as `manual`.

## Out of scope
- Importing a recipe from a web link or a cookbook photo (DESIGN §11, backlog 10).
- Recipe photos.
